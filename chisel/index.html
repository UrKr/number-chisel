Good to know

  • First to 12 points wins. Big targets are worth 4.
  • Carving can push two of your targets together: that's a combo, +1 extra.
  • Rivals have secret targets too. Watch what you build.
  • The Stone holds 12 digits at most (the dots under it).
  • Stuck? Tap Hint, or Swap to trade all your cards (uses your turn).
