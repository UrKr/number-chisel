How to play
  1. The Stone is one long number. On your turn, tap a digit, then tap a second digit to select the run between them.
  2. Pick one of your tools to strike that run. The run is replaced by the result. You see a preview before you confirm.
  3. After your strike, any of your secret commissions showing in the Stone are carved out and scored. A two-digit commission is worth 2 points and a three-digit one 4 points.
  4. Carving closes the gap, and the digits that become neighbours can form another of your commissions. Each extra carve in the same turn scores +1.
  5. The Stone can never be longer than 12 digits. If it drops below 4, random digits are added to its right end.
  6. Instead of striking, you can rest: swap all four tools for new ones. You still carve if a commission is showing.
  7. Your opponents' commissions are secret too. If your strike makes one of their numbers, they carve it on their turn. First to 12 points wins.
