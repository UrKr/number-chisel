New game

How to play

1

Your goal: make one of the gold numbers appear in the row of digits.

16in54172
2

Tap one of your cards, then tap a digit. The card changes that digit.

x²on 4 →516172
3

You made 16! It's carved out and you get a point. First to 5 points wins.

Helpers

  • A ★ on a card means it can score right now.
  • After you tap a card, bubbles show what each digit would become. Gold bubbles score.
  • Hint picks a move for you.

The cards

+1Adds one. 3 → 4, 9 → 10
−1Takes one away. 3 → 2
×2Doubles it. 7 → 14
x²Times itself. 4 → 16
✕Smashes the digit out of the row

You win!
