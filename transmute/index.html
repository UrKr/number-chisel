How to play

1

Brew a recipe by getting its two elements side by side on the shelf.

2

Tap one of your spell cards, then tap an element. The spell changes it.

3

Fire is now next to Water, so the pair is brewed and you score. First to 5 brews wins.

Helpers

  • A ★ on a card means it can brew a recipe right now.
  • After you tap a card, bubbles show what each element would become. Gold bubbles brew.
  • Hint picks a move for you.
